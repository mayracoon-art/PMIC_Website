Biography：

Tsung-Wen Sun was born in Kaohsiung City, Taiwan. He received the B.S. from the National Kaohsiung University of Science and Technology, Kaohsiung, Taiwan, in June 2019. In Jan. 2025, he also received the Ph.D. degree with the Department of Electrical Engineering, National Chung Cheng University, Taiwan. Moreover, he received the Annual Excellent Analog IC-Design Award from the TSRI, Taiwan, in 2023, the Excellence Award by the 23rd Macronix Golden Silicon Awards, and the 2023 IEEE SSCS Seoul Chapter Award, in 2023. In 2026, he become the Senior Analog Design Engineer at Realtek Corp., Taiwan. His research interests include analog circuit design for power management systems, readout integrated bbreak circuits, and biomedical circuits and systems.
